Apta2Switch-Studio — Start here

1. Keep this complete folder somewhere you can write, for example Documents.
   If you move it, move the whole Apta2Switch-Studio folder.

2. Double-click Apta2Switch-Studio.app on Mac, or Apta2Switch-Studio on Linux.
   The app opens in your browser.
   If macOS blocks the first launch, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security
   and choose Open Anyway.

3. Download NUPACK from https://www.nupack.org/downloads and extract its archive
   into the nupack folder. In the app, open Settings → Dependencies, confirm
   your NUPACK license, then click Validate NUPACK.

Your results are saved in runs/designs and runs/extensions by default.
Choose English or French in Settings → Language.

No Python installation or terminal commands are needed.
NUPACK is supplied separately under its own license.
